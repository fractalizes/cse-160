notes to grader:

your goal in this game is to find all the gold blocks inside the world by breaking the blocks underneath you. there are a total of eight gold blocks in the world and there is a counter at the top right to help you keep track of how many you found

controls:

  • click the game with [LMB] to begin, [ESC] to exit
  • [LMB] break block
  • [RMB] place block
  • [WASD] move forward, left, backward, right
  • [1-9] select block to place down (hotbar)
